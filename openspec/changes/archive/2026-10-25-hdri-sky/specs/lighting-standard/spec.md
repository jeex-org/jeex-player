## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Neutral environment

The standard SHALL provide its own deterministic, colourless environment, generated by the package from fixed parameters, so every consumer reflects and is lit by the same surroundings. Consumers SHALL NOT be able to substitute another environment through the standard's API. The player MAY put a host's HDRI on the scene over an attached apartment rig in walk and tour (capability `hdri-sky`); it SHALL put back the rig's environment, gain and rotation before the rig is detached.

#### Scenario: Same environment everywhere
- **WHEN** the environment is generated twice
- **THEN** both results have equal pixel values

#### Scenario: Colourless
- **WHEN** the environment is generated
- **THEN** every texel has equal red, green and blue values

#### Scenario: Orientation is the standard's
- **WHEN** a rig is attached to a scene whose environment rotation was changed
- **THEN** the scene's environment rotation is the identity while the rig is attached, and the previous rotation returns when it is detached

#### Scenario: Detach after an HDRI
- **WHEN** the viewer is disposed while an HDRI is live in walk
- **THEN** the HDRI is taken off first and the rig's detach restores the environment, gain and rotation the scene had before the rig

### Requirement: Published description and version

The entry SHALL publish a read-only description of the standard (engine revision, display transform, calibration target, environment parameters, rig values, sun presets, shadow tuning values, the baked-interior gains and the HDRI calibration values) and an appearance version number, raised with every intentional change of the look. The entry SHALL export the lighting error type and its codes.

#### Scenario: Description is read-only
- **WHEN** a consumer tries to change a value of the published description, at any depth
- **THEN** the value does not change

#### Scenario: Version
- **WHEN** a consumer reads the appearance version
- **THEN** it stays 1: the additions are data and functions that change no existing rig value

#### Scenario: Baked-interior gains
- **WHEN** a consumer reads `LIGHTING_STANDARD.baked.exposure`
- **THEN** it finds two positive gains, `editing` and `walk`, applied by the player to the lightmap of a baked surface only

#### Scenario: HDRI values
- **WHEN** a consumer reads `LIGHTING_STANDARD.hdri`
- **THEN** it finds positive numbers `environmentGain`, `loaderCompensation`, `skyBrightness` and `sunConeDeg`, and the recipe's `environment`, `apartment` and `reference` values are those of before this change

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: HDRI environment gain

The standard SHALL define the gain of an HDRI environment on the apartment rig as `environmentGain × loaderCompensation`, where `loaderCompensation` undoes the shipped decoder's measured darkening.

#### Scenario: Loader compensation measured
- **WHEN** the gain-map fixture of known linear radiance is decoded by the shipped decoder in the browser
- **THEN** the decoded mean, which may be darker, times `loaderCompensation` equals the known radiance within 2 %

### Requirement: HDRI sun intensity

The sun's normal irradiance SHALL be `max(0, A · share / (1 − share) − R) / sin(elevation)`, with A the decoded sky's horizontal irradiance outside a `sunConeDeg` cone around the stored sun and R the residual inside it; its intensity SHALL be that irradiance times the HDRI environment gain over the stored colour's luminance. A share of 1 SHALL be refused.

#### Scenario: Calibration on a known sky
- **WHEN** the calibration is applied to a synthetic sky of uniform radiance with share 0.5 and elevation 30°
- **THEN** the sun's normal irradiance equals max(0, A − R) / sin 30° within 1 %

#### Scenario: Share of 1
- **WHEN** an entry's share is 1
- **THEN** the calibration refuses it and the entry is dropped at option resolution
