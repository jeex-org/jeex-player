# Design

## Context

See proposal.md. Engine facts verified in three r186: grid choice by mesh origin with no binding API and a recompile when an object is in no grid; probes on the grid box surface; private bake camera; ranged bakes write into the live atlas; environment diffuse adds on top of the grid; grid and hemisphere counts in the program key; linear captures into targets.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** room grids, room probes with box projection, the walk rig, bake/refresh, restore.

**Non-Goals:** lamp lights (later step), HDRI sky (later step), quality tiers, grids in editing modes, captures of non-live states with room lighting.

## Decisions

1. **Modules** (walk chunk unless noted): `src/render/walk-lighting/room-volumes.ts` (pure), `room-grid.ts` (a `LightProbeGridWebGL` subclass with a selection box), `grid-bake.ts`, `room-probes.ts`, `box-probe-patch.ts`, `walk-rig.ts`; `src/geometry/shell-origins.ts` (post-build pass, every mode, not in the chunk); `src/app/walk-lighting-controller.ts` (lifecycle, modelled on the walk-post controller: generation counter, context lost/restored, one error report). Wiring lands in the controller and the stage; `render-runtime.ts` (664 lines) and `scene-builder.ts` (600) get at most a hook each and stay under 700.
2. **Volumes**: rectilinear split of the room polygon into boxes; sampling box = box inset 0.12 m (probes sit on its surface); selection box = the room box grown to the wall centreline, y from −0.2 m to ceiling + 0.2 m; projection box = the full room box; probe point = the interior point farthest from the room's edges at 1.5 m, nudged off furniture boxes. Grids are added as children of one parent in the required order (three pushes grids in traversal order and the first containing grid wins), so a door leaf's room (the side it opens into) comes first, which decides objects on a shared centreline. Up to 16 boxes in total; more is refused with a reported error and the direct look kept.
3. **Grid selection** (`room-grid.ts`): the grid's `boundingBox` is replaced by a `Box3` subclass whose `containsPoint` answers from a separate selection box, while `min`/`max` (read by the shader) stay the sampling box. three r186 selects a grid only through `boundingBox.containsPoint(objectOrigin)`; a browser test pins it (an object outside the sampling box but inside the selection box takes that grid's light). Furniture, wall-snapped assets, door leaves, window parts and opening frames are selected by their existing origins; their shared geometry is not touched.
4. **Shell origins**: shell meshes (zero origins, per-face geometry never shared) are rebased so the origin is the face centroid + 0.2 m along the inward normal (walls, skirtings), the polygon interior point at 0.1 m above the floor (floors) or 0.1 m below the ceiling (ceilings); joints to the room they border. Interior parametric walls are rebased in x/z only (the dollhouse owns `position.y`), opening frames in their group's local frame. Applied after the shell build and after `rebuildOpening` / `rebuildAll`. The slab and the site ground keep their positions.
5. **Exterior**: an analytic catch-all grid, 2×2×2, not baked, whose selection box answers `true` for every point: its texture is a small RGBA HalfFloat `Data3DTexture` in the probe-grid packing holding the hemisphere and the fill light projected into L2 spherical harmonics on the CPU (hemisphere: c0 = a/0.886227, c1 = b/1.023328; fill: clamped-cosine projection of its direction and colour). Fragments outside every room box keep the environment's diffuse on (the patch decides per fragment), so facades and the ground look as in the editing modes. Listed last; visible during pass 1 and during probe capture so facades seen through windows are lit in the bake.
6. **Bake**: room grids added to the scene with `visible = false` (a hidden grid is never pushed, so the screen keeps the direct look and partially written rows never show). Pass 0 of every grid with only the baking grid visible inside the call; pass 1 of every grid with all room grids visible inside the call (bounce through doorways; fixed order accepted); furniture, mirrors and transmissive meshes hidden inside each call and restored in `finally`. Spacing about 1 m, three height layers (with two, the probes sat under the sills and over the heads of the windows and a wall facing a window got about a quarter of its light, measured on the demo living room), `cubemapSize` 8; a fixed number of probes per frame (the testable budget). Bake starts only after the walk chain is ready.
7. **Rig states** (shared uniforms, no recompile): editing — environment diffuse on, box probes off; capture (grid bake and probe capture) — diffuse off inside room boxes, studio specular; walk — diffuse off inside room boxes, box probes on. The controller switches to the capture state around every bake or capture slice and back to the editing state for the frames in between, which still show the direct look. Hemisphere and fill at intensity 0 in capture and walk; the ambient floor on in walk only; walk exposure through `renderer.toneMappingExposure` (read by the chain's output pass, stills and panoramas), applied at the swap frame, restored on exit, re-applied after a context restore.
8. **Probes**: captured after pass 1, in the capture rig state, with grids visible inside the call (this also compiles every program the walk chain needs: the six faces of a capture draw every object, the exterior included, so no extra warm-up render is made); `PMREMGenerator.fromScene` at 128 px per capture (its own target, released after the copy), copied into one atlas of 384×512 tiles (columns beyond 8 rooms, under 4096); mirrors hidden; one room per frame. A run-time check fails loudly if the probe's PMREM cube size differs from the studio environment's.
9. **Patch**: one `onBeforeCompile` with a constant `customProgramCacheKey`, installed by a scene traversal on every bake trigger and on every new root or material (paint, upgrades, rebuilds), with `needsUpdate = true` on a material that was already compiled; replaces the environment-lighting chunk: diffuse × uIblDiffuse; specular from a copied `bilinearCubeUV` with a tile offset and the atlas texel size, room chosen per fragment from uniform arrays, lookup pushed 5 cm along the normal, ray–box parallax, smoothstep blend within 0.4 m of a shared box face; a fragment outside every room box uses the studio environment; clearcoat included; the anisotropy and retroreflection branches kept. With box probes off and diffuse on the output equals the unpatched shader. Concave rooms project one probe per sub-box: an accepted approximation. The specular multi-scattering term takes the grid's irradiance in place of the environment's, so rough metals keep their energy compensation.
10. **Swap**: after probes, all grids visible in one frame, rig to the walk state.
11. **Refresh** (debounced 1 s): a furniture edit re-captures its room's probe; a finish edit re-bakes its room's grid and probe; an opening edit re-bakes both adjoining rooms; a scene load, `rebuildAll`, a sun change or a context restore re-bakes everything. Captures requested during a bake use the direct look.
